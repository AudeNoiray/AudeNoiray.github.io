In the past decade, computer-based therapy has shown promise in speech rehabilitation for individuals with language disorders. Celise Haldin lead a recent collaborative study investigating behavioural, acoustic and brain network changes in a patient with chronic non-fluent aphasia. The study tested an enriched rehabilitation programme, combining the Ultraspeech-player software with conventional speech therapy.
