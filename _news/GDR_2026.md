Mathilde Fort and I organised the 2026 edition of the "Rencontre des BabyLabs de France", at Université Grenoble Alpes.
The GDR was a great success, gathering over 130 researchers and students from various labs in France. The event was preceded by the workshop "Developmental AI for Language and Cognition" organised by Thomas Hueber & Mathilde Fort.
