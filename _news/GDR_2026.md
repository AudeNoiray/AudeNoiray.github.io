Together with Mathilde Fort, I was delighted to take part in the organisation of the 2026 edition of the "Rencontre des BabyLabs de France", at université Grenoble Alpes.
The GDR was a great success, gathering over 130 participants from various labs in France. The event was preceded by the workshop "Developmental AI for Language and Cognition" an initiative lead by Thomas Hueber & Mathilde Fort.
